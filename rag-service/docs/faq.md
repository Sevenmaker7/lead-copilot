# Orbit Automation — FAQ

## How fast can you start?

We typically start the discovery call within 3 business days of first contact, and begin building
within a week of a signed scope.

## Do you work with our existing systems?

Yes — we integrate with whatever you already use (CRM, ERP, spreadsheets, messaging platforms) via
their APIs rather than asking you to switch tools.

## What if the automation makes a mistake?

Anything customer-facing or high-stakes (sending a reply, updating a financial record) goes through
a human approval step by default. You decide what level of autonomy you're comfortable with, and we
can loosen it over time as trust builds.

## Do you offer a trial or pilot?

The Starter Automation package (from $1,200) is effectively a scoped pilot — one workflow, fixed
price, fast delivery, so you can evaluate the quality of our work before a larger engagement.

## What industries do you work with?

Mostly e-commerce, finance/accounting teams, and B2B services companies, but our automations are
industry-agnostic — the pattern (manual repetitive task → automated pipeline with AI where judgment
is needed) applies broadly.

## Do you offer refunds?

If a Starter Automation isn't delivered as scoped within the agreed timeline, we refund the
engagement in full. Growth Package and AI Agent Build refunds are prorated based on milestones
completed.
