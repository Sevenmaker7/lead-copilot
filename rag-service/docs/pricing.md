# Orbit Automation — Pricing

All engagements are fixed-scope, fixed-price after a free 30-minute discovery call. We do not do
open-ended hourly billing.

## Starter Automation — from $1,200

One workflow automated end-to-end (e.g. lead intake and routing, invoice data extraction). Delivered
in 1-2 weeks. Includes 30 days of support after go-live.

## Growth Package — from $3,500

Up to 4 connected automations sharing a common data layer (e.g. lead intake + qualification +
CRM sync + reporting). Delivered in 3-4 weeks. Includes 60 days of support.

## AI Agent Build — from $2,500

A single-purpose AI agent (chatbot, document processor, internal assistant) including a human
review/approval step, deployed to Telegram, WhatsApp, or your internal tools. Delivered in 2-3 weeks.

## Ongoing Care Plan — $300/month

Monitoring, prompt/rubric tuning, and up to 3 hours of changes per month for automations we've
already built for you.

Custom/enterprise scopes are quoted individually after discovery — we do not publish enterprise
pricing here.
