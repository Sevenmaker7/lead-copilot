# Orbit Automation — Services

Orbit Automation is a boutique AI automation agency. We design and ship automations that remove
manual, repetitive work from finance, sales, and operations teams.

## Business Process Automation

We map a team's manual workflow (spreadsheet copying, manual data entry between systems, repetitive
email triage) and replace it with an automated pipeline using n8n/Make plus AI where judgment is
needed. Typical engagement: 2-4 weeks from discovery to a live automation.

## AI Agents & Chatbots

We build AI agents that handle a defined task end-to-end — lead qualification, document processing,
internal Q&A over your company's knowledge base — with a human approval step for anything
customer-facing or high-stakes. Deployed as Telegram/WhatsApp bots or embedded widgets.

## Document & Data Processing

OCR and LLM-based extraction for invoices, contracts, and reports — turning unstructured PDFs and
scans into structured data in your CRM, ERP, or spreadsheet of choice.

## Integration & CRM/ERP Connectivity

We connect AI automations to the systems you already use — Bitrix24, HubSpot, 1C, Airtable, Google
Workspace — via their APIs, so automations fit into your existing operations rather than replacing
them.
